Your information and choices

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

This policy explains how BlipLink handles information when you visit our website, create or manage a digital ID card, or exchange contact details through a card. BlipLink is the service name; contact our team at devops@shaunco.ai with privacy questions or requests.

Information we handle

Account information

When an account is created or invited, BlipLink uses your email address and account authentication information to provide sign-in and access. Sign-in is provided through Supabase Auth. We do not receive your plaintext password.

Card and company information

Information you choose to add may include your name, job title, organization, biography, email, phone number, address, website and social links, wallet-pass links, profile photo, cover image, design settings, and company or team details. A public card displays the information and links configured for that card. Anyone with its link may view and share it, and search engines or other people may copy or retain public information.

Contact exchanges

If a visitor uses Exchange contact on a public card, we collect the name and phone number they submit, plus any optional email, company, role, services, and message. We also record the card owner, the card associated with the request, the submission time, and the visitor’s consent to share the details. The card owner can view the submitted information in their BlipLink account. Do not submit another person’s details without permission.

Card activity

When a public card is viewed or a link or button is tapped, BlipLink records the event type, card, and time for the card owner’s analytics. Where available, the hosting request supplies a city and country estimate. The analytics event sent to our database does not include a full IP address; our hosting providers may separately process connection data in operational logs.

Website and browser storage

Our website stores your language choice in browser local storage. Signed-in areas use browser storage for the authentication session and notification state. These are used to provide requested site functions; the site does not use these items to build advertising profiles.

How we use information

  • Provide, secure, and maintain BlipLink accounts, public cards, team workspaces, and card settings.
  • Deliver contact-exchange submissions to the card owner who was shown to the visitor.
  • Show card activity to the account owner and help them manage follow-ups.
  • Respond to support requests, diagnose errors, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

When information is shared

  • Public card visitors: card information and links you choose to publish are available to people who open the public card.
  • Card owners: contact details submitted through Exchange contact are delivered to the owner of the selected card.
  • Service providers: Supabase provides authentication, database, and file-storage services; Vercel hosts the website and processes card-activity requests. Google Fonts may receive standard browser request information when fonts load. QR codes are generated through QRServer using the page or card URL needed to create the code.
  • Links you choose: opening a social, wallet, email, phone, or other external link sends information to the destination you selected, under that provider’s own terms and privacy policy.
  • Legal and safety reasons: information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect people, or secure the service.

Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours. Their handling is governed by their own terms and our service arrangements.

Retention and security

We keep account, card, exchange, and activity information while it is needed to provide and secure the service, handle support, and meet legal obligations. If you want information corrected or deleted, contact us; we will review the request and explain any information we must retain. We use access controls and other safeguards designed to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

  • Edit or remove information from your card in your account. Remember that information already viewed, copied, or cached by others may persist.
  • Choose whether to submit a contact-exchange form. The form requires consent before sending its details to the card owner.
  • Contact devops@shaunco.ai to ask about access, correction, deletion, or other privacy choices that may apply where you live.

We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Some requests may be limited by legal requirements or the rights of other people.

Children

BlipLink is a professional networking service and is not designed to collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through BlipLink, contact us so we can review it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date above shows when the current version took effect. We will post material updates on this page.

Privacy contact: devops@shaunco.ai. This policy describes current product behavior and does not reduce any rights that cannot legally be limited.
